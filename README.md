# HR Analytics Dashboard Using Power BI

## Project Objectives
To develop an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.
To analyze employee data and identify workforce trends.
To monitor key HR metrics such as total employees, attrition rate, and active employees.
To analyze employee attrition based on department, job role, education, and age group.
To visualize employee demographics, including gender and age distribution.
To evaluate salary distribution and its impact on employee attrition.
To create interactive dashboards using charts, KPIs, and slicers for better data exploration.
To transform raw HR data into meaningful insights through data visualization.
To support data-driven decision-making for HR professionals and management.
To improve reporting efficiency by presenting HR data in a clear and interactive format.

## Project Process

Collected the HR employee dataset from the provided data source.
Imported the dataset into Microsoft Power BI.
Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query by handling missing values, correcting data types, and removing unnecessary columns.
Created calculated columns and DAX measures to calculate key HR metrics such as Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, Active Employees, and Average Age.
Designed the data model to ensure accurate relationships and efficient analysis.
Developed interactive visualizations including KPI cards, bar charts, pie charts, donut charts, and slicers.
Analyzed employee data to identify trends in attrition, demographics, salary, education, and job roles.
Generated actionable business insights to support HR decision-making and employee retention strategies.
Tested the dashboard to ensure all visuals, filters, and calculations worked correctly.
Published the completed project to GitHub with the Power BI file, project documentation, dashboard screenshots, and README file.

## Project Insights
The organization has an overall employee attrition rate that highlights the need for improved employee retention strategies.
Employees in certain departments experience higher attrition than others, indicating areas that require focused HR attention.
Employees in the 26–35 years age group represent a significant portion of the workforce and contribute to a large share of attrition.
Lower salary ranges are associated with higher employee turnover, suggesting that compensation may influence retention.
Some job roles show consistently higher attrition, indicating the need for role-specific engagement and career development programs.
Most employees have a background in Life Sciences and Medical education fields, reflecting the organization's workforce composition.
The workforce consists of both male and female employees, with a balanced distribution that supports diversity analysis.
Interactive filters and visualizations enable HR teams to analyze employee trends across multiple dimensions, supporting faster and more informed decisions.

## Final Conclusion

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of workforce performance and employee attrition through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators. By analyzing employee demographics, salary, job roles, education, and departmental trends, the dashboard helps HR professionals identify the major factors affecting employee retention. These insights support data-driven decision-making, allowing the organization to improve employee satisfaction, reduce attrition, optimize workforce planning, and develop effective HR strategies.
